1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Higher education analytics systems historically assess students using siloed academic and transactional attributes—predominantly grade point averages, credit completion velocity, and lecture attendance logs. However, sociological research demonstrates that student retention, emotional well-being, collaborative problem solving, and campus belonging are fundamentally governed by informal social networks.

CampusConnect models campus interactions as a complex relational graph of 630 students across 7 academic departments with 3,985 recorded mutual friendship ties. By coupling NetworkX graph-theoretic routines with an interactive React and Cytoscape.js dashboard, the platform reveals emergent social communities, identifies opinion leaders, detects structural bridges, and isolates disconnected students who require targeted student affairs outreach.

3. Graph Algorithms & Mathematical Formulations

3.1 Louvain Community Detection (Modularity Maximization)

The platform organizes students into natural peer groups by maximizing Newman-Girvan Modularity Q:

Q = (1 / 2m) * Σ [ A_ij - γ * (k_i * k_j) / (2m) ] * δ(c_i, c_j) Where: - m = 3,985 (total edges) - γ = 0.75 (modularity resolution parameter) - A_ij = 1 if friendship exists, 0 otherwise - k_i, k_j = node degrees - δ(c_i, c_j) = 1 if nodes share community, 0 otherwise

The algorithm executes two repeating phases: local modularity optimization followed by hierarchical community aggregation. It uncovers 4 emergent communities with a modularity of Q = 0.6777:

  • Community 1: 179 students (28.4% of cohort)
  • Community 2: 166 students (26.3% of cohort)
  • Community 3: 152 students (24.1% of cohort)
  • Community 4: 127 students (20.2% of cohort)

3.2 Centrality Metrics & Algorithmic Roles

Metric Mathematical Formulation Algorithmic Role
Degree Centrality C_D(v) = deg(v) / (N - 1) Measures immediate local popularity and direct social sphere size.
PageRank (α = 0.85) PR(u) = (1-α)/N + α * Σ [PR(v) / deg(v)] Measures recursive structural prestige and opinion leadership.
Betweenness Centrality C_B(v) = Σ [σ_st(v) / σ_st] (Brandes) Measures shortest-path mediation to detect cross-community bridge students.
Closeness Centrality C_C(v) = (N - 1) / Σ d(v, u) Measures average proximity to determine rapid information disseminators.

5. Overview of Core Functional Modules

  1. Overview Dashboard: High-level KPIs, community distribution bar charts, departmental activity, and primary navigation actions.
  2. Student Network Explorer: Interactive Cytoscape.js graph with layout selection (CoSE, Concentric, Circle, Grid), color/size modes, and node inspection drawers.
  3. Louvain Communities Explorer: Deep-dive profiles of all 4 communities, internal edge density, dominant departments, top hubs, and inter-community interaction matrix.
  4. Influential Students: Leaderboards ranked by PageRank and degree centrality with department filtering and CSV export.
  5. Bridge Students Detection: Identifies shortest-path bottlenecks and boundary spanners connecting disparate communities.
  6. Completely Isolated Students: Specialized intervention dashboard tracking the 6 singletons with degree zero.
  7. Four-Level Connectivity Analysis: Mutually exclusive tier distributions and intervention tracking table for low-connectivity students.
  8. Department Analytics: Comparative department metrics, internal vs external friendship ratios, and 7x7 interaction matrix.
  9. Centrality Metric Comparison: Scatter correlation plots (Degree vs PageRank, Degree vs Betweenness) and degree histograms.
  10. Data Explorer & Validation: Raw node/edge tables, real-time schema validation reports, and CSV download center.
  11. Methodology & Insights: Scientific documentation detailing mathematical formulations, parameter choices, and ethical data guidelines.
